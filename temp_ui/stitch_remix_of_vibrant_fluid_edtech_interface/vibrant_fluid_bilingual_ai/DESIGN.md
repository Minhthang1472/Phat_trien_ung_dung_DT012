## Brand & Style

This design system powers an AI-driven bilingual video learning platform tailored for playful, immersive mobile education. The brand embodies joyful discovery, effortless language switching, and intelligent guidance without corporate friction. The interface projects high-energy warmth, safety, and modern pedagogical delight, motivating learners through micro-achievements and fluid media transitions.

The visual direction merges **Vibrant Playful Aesthetics** with **Fluid Tech Glassmorphism**:
- **Organic Fluidity**: Wavy color ribbons, pill contours, and energetic squiggles provide friendly momentum through modular video units and dual-subtitle tracks.
- **Atmospheric Aura Glows**: Instead of heavy industrial drop shadows, surfaces float above soft, translucent color fields matching primary and tertiary accents.
- **Glass Translucency & Tactility**: Secondary layers, floating video control bars, and contextual translation sheets utilize frosted multi-layered surfaces over warm canvas backdrops.

## Colors

The palette balances energetic tropical accents with high-legibility dark ink tones over a comforting, warm-white substrate.

- **Canvas & Neutrals**: 
  - Base Background: `#F8F9FE` (Warm Cloud White) transitioning to `#FAFAFC` for secondary sheet tiers.
  - Surface Neutral: `#FFFFFF` for primary cards, elevated containers, and interactive chips.
  - Deep Ink Typography: `#1E1B4B` (Midnight Indigo) for primary headlines and heavy weights; `#475569` (Slate Ink) for supportive instructional body copy.
  - Ghost Borders: `rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.06)` for gentle structural definition.

- **Chromatic Pillars**:
  - **Primary (Tropical Coral)**: `#FF6F61` with a lighter fluid tint `#FF8A65`. Governs primary actions, key video scrub heads, and AI conversational triggers.
  - **Secondary (Mint Turquoise)**: `#06D6A0` with depth `#2EC4B6`. Signals correct answers, active listening status, comprehension milestones, and native language toggles.
  - **Tertiary (Lavender Violet)**: `#7B2CBF` with vibrant highlight `#9D4EDD`. Anchors deep AI intelligence tags, grammar breakdown cards, and audio speed settings.
  - **Accent Sunshine (Lemon)**: `#FFD166` (Bright Sunshine) and `#FFE066`. Used for streak flames, star badges, attention-grabbing phoneme markers, and praise banners.
  - **Accent Pulse (Neon Powder Pink)**: `#FF5C8A` to `#F72585`. Highlights immediate vocabulary bookmarks, pronunciation retries, and high-energy celebrations.

## Typography

The design system uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all text levels. Its geometric structure, open apertures, and friendly counters deliver effortless scanability during high-speed video playback and split-screen language comparison.

- **Bilingual Subtitle Pairing**: Dual-line video captions enforce strict contrast: the target learning language uses `body-lg` at weight `700` in deep midnight ink (`#1E1B4B`), while the translated scaffolding language directly underneath renders in `body-sm` at weight `500` in Slate Ink (`#475569`).
- **Interactive Phonemes**: Tappable vocabulary words within transcripts carry a subtle 2px dotted coral underline (`#FF6F61`), scaling with line-height without displacing vertical text cadence.
- **Numbers and Badges**: XP counts, speech-matching accuracy scores, and streaks use `headline-md` or `label-lg` with tight letter tracking (`-0.02em`) to heighten gameful energy.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for fluid mobile-first thumb interaction, leveraging a vertically driven modular grid.

- **Grid Architecture**: Mobile viewports use a 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Tablet and foldables step up to a 6- or 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters to permit side-by-side video feed and interactive vocabulary boards.
- **Video Learning Canvas**: The primary media view stays locked at a 16:9 ratio for full-bleed clip discovery, or snaps to an adaptive 4:5 vertical reel format. Media controls float with minimum safe zone clearances of `1.25rem` from the screen perimeter.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm**: Strict adherence to the `0.5rem` base unit ensures that subtitle stacks, translation accordions, and speed controls maintain airiness without forcing content past the natural thumb zone.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses colored aura glows and luminous frosted glass planes instead of muddy grayscale shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat/Base)**: `#F8F9FE` canvas backdrop. Wavy vector illustrations and soft color squiggles sit directly on this level with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces with subtle border `1px solid rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.05)` and an ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating)**: Active vocabulary pills, sticky lesson headers, and selected cards: `0 12px 32px -6px rgba(255, 111, 97, 0.18)` (Primary Coral Aura) or `0 12px 32px -6px rgba(6, 214, 160, 0.20)` (Mint Aura for solved states).
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & AI Drawers)**: Frosted backdrop layers using `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, and `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -8px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a pill-shaped, ultra-soft shape hierarchy (`roundedness: 3`), reinforcing approachable learning psychology.

- **Pill Form Elements (`rounded-full`)**: Applied to all primary CTA buttons, language switchers, audio playback pills, and vocabulary tag chips.
- **Large Container Surfaces (`rounded-3xl` / 2rem)**: Used on video player frames, AI assistant speech bubbles, and pop-up challenge modals.
- **Standard Cards (`rounded-2xl` / 1.25rem)**: Used on lesson modules, definition drawers, and audio waveform containers.
- **Fluid Wave Accents**: SVG dividers, progress headers, and dynamic milestone ribbons use asymmetric rounded curves to guide eye pathing toward next steps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Coral Bounce)**: Full pill shape (`rounded-full`), solid `#FF6F61` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `label-lg`. Elevated by a tropical glow (`0 8px 20px -2px rgba(255, 111, 97, 0.35)`). Active state applies a spring transform (`scale(0.97)`).
- **Secondary AI Action (Violet Glow)**: Pill shape, `#7B2CBF` fill with crisp white text, accompanied by dynamic glowing particle micro-interactions on generation states.
- **Ghost & Subtle Controls**: Pure white pill with `1.5px solid rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.08)`, text `#1E1B4B`, shifting to soft lemon background (`#FFF8E7`) on hover/touch.

### Chips & Badges
- **Bilingual Language Toggle**: Dual-segmented pill with a sliding thumb. Active language is filled with `#06D6A0` (Mint) and `#FFFFFF` text; inactive language is muted `#475569`.
- **Vocabulary Difficulty Badges**: Pill-shaped micro-tags using soft pastel tint fills paired with deep saturated text:
  - Beginner: `#E6FBF5` fill, `#048A67` text.
  - Intermediate: `#FFF9E6` fill, `#B38600` text.
  - Advanced: `#F3E8FF` fill, `#7B2CBF` text.

### Interactive Video Cards
- **Video Feed Tile**: `rounded-3xl` container clipping the media cleanly. Overlaid at the top-right is an ambient glass badge (`rgba(255,255,255,0.75)`) displaying video CEFR levels.
- **AI Dual-Subtitle Display**: Floating frosted glass card anchored at bottom-center of playback viewport. Words are segmented into individually tappable touch targets with smooth active highlights (`#FFE066`).

### Inputs & Pronunciation Mic
- **Text & Search Fields**: Pill-shaped containers (`rounded-full`), `#FFFFFF` background, `1.5px solid rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.08)`, inner padding `0.875rem 1.5rem`. Focus ring renders as a vivid `#FF6F61` aura without sharp browser outlines.
- **Speech Practice Button**: Floating circular button (64px × 64px) with multi-layered pulse wave animations in mint turquoise (`#06D6A0`), expanding outward to show real-time mic sensitivity.

### Lists & Selection Modules
- **Quiz Choice Rows**: Large, touch-friendly cards (`rounded-2xl`), minimum height 56px, containing left-aligned alphabet badges and right-aligned phonetic hints. Selection triggers an animated fill transition to either `#06D6A0` (success) or `#FF5C8A` (retry needed) with energetic spring physics.
